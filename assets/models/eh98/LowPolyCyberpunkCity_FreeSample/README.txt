LOW POLY CYBERPUNK CITY — FREE SAMPLE
=====================================

Five models from the full Low Poly Cyberpunk City pack, free to use
in commercial projects.

WHAT IS INSIDE
  vending_machine  lit display, goods on the shelves, glowing head panel
  noodle_cart      counter, tarp, pots, stools, hanging lanterns
  street_lamp      neon tube instead of a lampshade
  sign_tall        vertical neon sign with an abstract glyph field
  road_tile        12 m wet asphalt tile with markings, puddles and drains

FORMATS
  Every asset ships as .blend, .fbx, .obj (+ .mtl) and .glb.

STYLE
  Flat-shaded low poly, solid colours, NO textures and no UV work.
  Neon is emissive geometry: separate tubes, sign faces and light
  strips. Nothing relies on a texture map.

  Enable bloom or a glow post-process in your engine. Without it
  emissive surfaces read as flat colour rather than light.

SCALE AND ORIGINS
  Real-world scale in metres, origins at the base of each object.
  The road tile is 12 m square and tiles seamlessly.

THE FULL PACK
  35 models — megablock towers, a shopfront with a lit interior, a
  noodle bar, an arcade, a capsule hotel, a corporate tower, signs,
  street modules, hover vehicles — plus a Blender add-on that
  generates whole districts and a demo scene.

LICENCE
  Royalty-free, commercial use allowed including games you sell.
  You may modify the models. You may not resell or redistribute the
  assets as an asset pack.

AI DISCLOSURE
  The models are generated by a Python script that builds the geometry
  procedurally. The script was written with AI assistance. No image
  generator or 3D generator produced the meshes.
