ABOUT ME

Who is Tiyah?

Game designer & developer

The person behind the pixels

I'm Tiyah, a 23-year-old creative with a passion for art, design and making things that didn't exist before. I've always been someone who loves creating, experimenting with ideas and finding ways to bring the things in my head into the real world. But what I find even more exciting is that my work doesn't have to stop with me. I get to put something I've imagined out into the world for someone else to experience, interact with and hopefully connect with. Whether it's making someone feel something, see things from a different perspective, spark their curiosity or learn something new, I want what I create to leave a little something behind. There's something pretty amazing about knowing that something you made could mean something to someone else.

Chapter 01

A little detour...

My journey into game development wasn't exactly a straight line. I initially pursued a BSc in Biomedicine, spending two years exploring a completely different field before realising I wanted something that allowed me to exercise my creativity alongside my technical interests. I wanted to design, experiment, build and bring ideas to life, which eventually led me to take a leap in a completely different direction.

Chapter 02

Finding my world at Vega

My journey as a game developer began at Vega School, where I discovered just how much could be achieved through the combination of programming, art and design. Each year introduced me to new ways of creating interactive experiences, from developing gameplay mechanics and programming systems to crafting visual identities, immersive environments and meaningful narratives. It was where I began to understand that game development was the perfect intersection of my technical and creative interests.

Chapter 03

More than just a game

One of the biggest things I've learnt is that games have the potential to be so much more than entertainment. They can teach, challenge perspectives, spark conversations and create emotional connections. I'm particularly drawn to the idea of creating experiences that leave players with something to think about long after they've put down the controller. Whether through storytelling, gameplay or visual communication, I believe every element of a game has the potential to say something.

Chapter 04

The best of both worlds

I aspire to grow as a well-rounded game developer, with a particular interest in gameplay programming and C# development alongside UI/UX design and visual development. I'm equally fascinated by the logic behind how a game functions and the creative decisions that shape how it looks, feels and is experienced. I'm drawn to the challenge of taking an idea from its initial concept and visual direction all the way through to a fully functioning, interactive experience. Why choose between the art and the code when I can bring both together?

Chapter 05

Beyond the screen

When I'm not working on a project, I'm usually finding inspiration in other games, exploring different visual styles, experimenting with creative ideas or getting carried away with a new project. I'm naturally drawn to the little details that give things personality, whether it's an interesting colour palette, a beautifully designed interface or an entire fictional world. I believe inspiration can come from just about anywhere, and I'm always collecting ideas for what I could create next.
