Don't Forget to Say Goodbye

Horror interactive LLM game · Unity 6

Team: Tiyah Singh and David Barker

Fiction about grief, death, and the occult. Aimed at players 16 and older.

Project brief

This project focused on developing a small-scale interactive game or system that explores the use of locally hosted Large Language Models (LLMs) in game design. It aimed to create meaningful AI-enhanced player experiences through features such as adaptive dialogue, procedural storytelling, or intelligent interactions, while targeting players interested in innovative, AI-driven interactive experiences.

Team credits

Team: Tiyah Singh and David Barker

Game synopsis

In Don't Forget to Say GOODBYE, players assume the role of a widower seeking answers after his wife mysteriously disappears following her involvement with occult rituals. Using a spirit board, players communicate with an AI-driven spirit while completing ritual puzzles to uncover the truth behind her disappearance. As paranormal interference escalates, they must maintain the protective ritual and ultimately find her grave to achieve closure.
