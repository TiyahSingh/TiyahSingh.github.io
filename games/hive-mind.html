Hive Mind

Possession puzzle · Unity · HTML5

Credits: Tiyah Singh

Project brief

The prototype brief was a first-person puzzle about possession: the witch cannot open the doors herself, so the solution is to spend mana, use the skeleton, and still escape in her own body.

Team credits

Tiyah Singh

Game synopsis

Hive Mind is published on itch.io as Hex Mind. You play a necromancy witch in a locked dungeon. Stand near a door, possess the skeleton bound to it, and walk that skeleton onto a pressure plate. Mana drains while you are inside a skeleton. If it runs out, you are thrown back into your own body and lose a life. Boxes can hold a plate down after you leave.
