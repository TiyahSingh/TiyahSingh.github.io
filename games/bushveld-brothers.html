Bushveld Brothers

Ranger adventure · Unity · C#

Synopsis

Bushveld Brothers is a short 3D ranger game set in the South African bushveld. You patrol as Barries, follow tracks, rescue wildlife, and stop poachers before the clock runs out. The ending is an arrest, not a kill.

Project brief

The project began as a YellowDot game-jam prototype and was then developed as a Starr Studios commercial concept: a premium, short Steam adventure about hunting the hunters. The brief asked for a readable patrol loop, a South African setting, and a product that could be pitched with a game design document, interface system, and store artwork.

Role and contribution

Tiyah Singh produced the project and led the interface work. On the studio roster she is project manager, lead UI/UX designer, gameplay programmer, and documentation lead. She kept the schedule, designed menus and HUD flow, implemented gameplay in C#, and wrote the production records.

Starr Studios: Tiyah Singh, Jamie-Lee Davies, and Barrie George Davies.
