Bushveld Brothers

Ranger adventure · Unity

Credits: Tiyah Singh, David Barker, Barrie-George Davies, Jamie-Lee Davies

Project brief

Made in three days for a YellowDot Africa campus jam on the theme Games for Africa: Stories, Cultures and Connections, with a mobile-first requirement. The jam prototype was later treated as a Starr Studios commercial concept for a premium short Steam adventure: a readable patrol loop, South African bushveld identity, Ubuntu-centred non-lethal conflict, and store-ready pitch / UI / documentation artefacts.

Team credits

Tiyah Singh, David Barker, Barrie-George Davies, Jamie-Lee Davies

Team: Starr Studios / Games for Africa jam team — Tiyah Singh, David Barker (Omega0), Barrie-George Davies (TheBgster), Jamie-Lee Davies.

Game synopsis

Bushveld Brothers is a short 3D ranger adventure set in the South African bushveld. You play Barries on a timed patrol: read spoor, free snared animals, and detain poachers before sunset. The fantasy inverts hunting games — animals are wards, poachers are the quarry, and the ending is an arrest, not a kill.
