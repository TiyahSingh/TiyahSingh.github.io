Echo's Lament

Mirror puzzle · Unity · HTML5

Synopsis

Echo's Lament is a short grid puzzle made for a jam on the theme Echo and Reflection. You steer Narcissus. Echo moves as his opposite: up becomes down, and left becomes right. Both of them must stand on their exits at the same time. A move that saves one can trap the other.

Project brief

The jam brief was to turn the theme into the rule of the game, using the myth of Narcissus and Echo. Reflection is not decoration. Every input is two inputs, and some levels limit how many moves you have.

Role and contribution

Tiyah is a credited author of the jam game with Jamie-Lee Davies and Barrie-George Davies. The public page lists the three of them as the contributors.
