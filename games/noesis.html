Noesis

Atmosphere prototype · Unity

A standalone trailer is not published. The repository is the public build.

Synopsis

Noesis, also titled Depth of the Mind, is a Unity prototype aimed at mood and exploration. The project is built with horror ambience, footsteps, and a hospital environment pack, and it is documented with a code reference list.

Project brief

The GADS concept brief was to take an idea called Depth of the Mind and turn it into a prototype with a cited bibliography, rather than a finished commercial game.

Role and contribution

Tiyah is a contributor on the Depth repository with Jamie-Lee Davies and David Barker. Her work sits in that shared prototype and its reference documentation.
