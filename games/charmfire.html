Charmfire

Action prototype · Unreal Engine 5

A separate gameplay video is not published. The repository is both the demo record and the download.

Synopsis

Charmfire is a combat prototype in which the player sprints, fights, and can fall, while bots patrol, attack, flee, and look for health. Weapons do not share one behaviour: a pistol hits immediately, and a grenade launcher fires a projectile.

Project brief

The GADE brief was an Unreal Engine 5 game whose combat and AI could grow without collapsing into one giant script. The team separated actions into states and decisions into strategies so new behaviour could be added without rewriting player input.

Role and contribution

Tiyah co-developed the Unreal project with Jamie-Lee Davies. Both developers filed individual documentation in the repository. The gameplay is built in Blueprints, with state objects for movement and combat and strategy objects for bot decisions.

Tiyah Singh and Jamie-Lee Davies.
