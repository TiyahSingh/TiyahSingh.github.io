A Cat's Tale

Grid adventure · C# · Windows Forms

Project brief

The GADE brief was a C# game whose later stage raised the strategic pressure: more enemies, clearer feedback, and save points so a run could be continued. The map, level, rules, and buttons all live on one form.

Gameplay

A Cat's Tale is a grid adventure played through a Windows Forms map. You move with WASD or the arrow keys, avoid tiles that block or hurt you, and fight grunt enemies that wander and attack when they are next to you. Health pickups restore 10 hit points. Later parts add tougher enemy types, save and load, and a guide so the rules stay readable.

Technologies
