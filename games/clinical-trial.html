Credits: Tiyah Singh, L.A. van der Zee, Jamie-Lee Davies, Jacques Willemse

Rated 4.6/5 on itch.io from community playtests. GitHub: LJ-vdZ/XBCGD7312_Clinical-Trial.

Project brief

WIL / XBCGD7311–7312 serious-game brief: design and ship a 3D hospital simulation that raises awareness of systemic public-healthcare challenges (HAIs, overcrowding, staff shortages, corruption, outages, resource scarcity). Players must feel scarcity, switch roles, and keep the clinic running through planned work and sudden emergencies, with research-backed documentation and public itch.io / GitHub delivery.

Tiyah Singh, L.A. van der Zee, Jamie-Lee Davies, Jacques Willemse

Team: Clinical Trial Devs — Tiyah Singh, L.A. van der Zee (LazjessicahvdZ), Jamie-Lee Davies, Jacques Willemse (FeelGoodBinJuice).

Game synopsis

Clinical Trial is a 3D multi-role hospital management simulation about South African public healthcare under pressure. You switch between Manager, Doctor, Nurse, and Janitor to treat patients, scrub filth, balance the budget, and survive stolen medicine, power outages, and patient influxes while keeping sanitation, comfort, morale, and related clinic stats alive. One bad shift and the clinic shuts down.
