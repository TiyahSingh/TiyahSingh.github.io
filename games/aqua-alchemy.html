Synopsis

Aqua Alchemy is a GADS prototype about alchemy and water, presented to a client and built in Unity. The public record of the idea is the pitch presentation. The playable project, schedule, and reference list live in the repository.

The brief was a client-facing prototype: a pitch that explains the game, plus a Unity build, a project schedule, and a reference list that shows where the work came from.
