Dreamy Mosaics

Level prototype · Unity 6

Synopsis

Dreamy Mosaics is a Unity 6 level prototype. The player moves with WASD, carries a glow light, and plays toward win and lose screens. The build includes an about panel, a controls panel, brightness controls, and a mute button.

Project brief

The prototype brief was a small, readable level game: movement, feedback when you win or lose, and settings a player can find without leaving the level.

Role and contribution

The public commit history attributes the implementation to Jamie-Lee Davies and Lazjessicah van der Zee. Tiyah is presenting the prototype with the rest of the studio's released work. This page does not invent a task split that the repository does not record.
