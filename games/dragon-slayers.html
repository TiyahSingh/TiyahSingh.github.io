Project brief

The GADE5121 brief was a C# Windows Forms game made in a pair, with a schedule, a reference list, a written game report, and a recorded presentation of the finished part.

Gameplay

Dragon Slayers is a Windows Forms battle. The opening form starts the fight and hands the turn to the player. The GADE project grew across three parts, from the first forms to a finished battle build, a game report, and a presentation.
