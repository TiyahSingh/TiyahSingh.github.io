# Don't Forget to Say GOODBYE

A haunted manor. A spirit board. An AI that answers back.

Play as a widower seeking answers after his wife vanishes into occult ritual. Speak through the Ouija board to a locally driven spirit, solve ritual puzzles, keep the candles lit, and find her grave before the séance collapses.

## Features
- Diegetic Ouija board conversations powered by a custom AI dialogue system
- Ritual puzzles, tarot, and escalating paranormal interference
- Horror atmosphere with authored plot gates so the story stays on track

## Notes
Fiction about grief, death, and the occult. Aimed at players 16+.
